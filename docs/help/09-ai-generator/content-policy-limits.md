# AI content policy and limits

Use the generator for ordinary prototype interaction drafts. The service can reject prompts that are outside its safety rules, too large, ambiguous, or unsupported. Availability also depends on network access, service limits, and credits.

Keep prompts focused on the UI behavior you want. Avoid private project content, credentials, personal information, or material you are not authorized to send to an external service.

If a prompt fails, simplify it or build the interaction with the rule editor. The generator is not a source of guaranteed correctness; always inspect its output and test the interaction.
