# Free tier and credits

Creating screens, editing widgets, saving `.pflow` files, using the local preview, and exporting a local HTML bundle are local workflows; they do not consume wallet credits. Some optional cloud-backed actions, such as AI generation, collaboration, or publishing, may require credits and an internet connection.

The wallet shows your current balance, recent activity, and refill status. Daily refills are subject to the wallet cap and require a connection. Credit costs are shown in the relevant action before it runs; check the current UI rather than relying on an old estimate.

An account is optional for local authoring. Account-linked features explain their sign-in requirement in the relevant dialog. Never include private project or customer data in public issue reports.
