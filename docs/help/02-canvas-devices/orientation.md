# Orientation toggle

Open Canvas view options and choose **Switch to portrait** or **Switch to landscape**. The orientation change swaps the device viewport dimensions. When widgets may overflow the new viewport, ProtoFlow tells you how many are affected and offers to keep their geometry or rotate the widgets too.

Keeping widgets as-is preserves their coordinates and dimensions, which is useful when you want to rearrange the layout manually. Rotating widgets applies a rotation to the selected screen content along with the device change.

Review the result at the intended breakpoint. Undo is available if the orientation change or widget rotation is not what you expected.
